Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

ClipEngine is a desktop tool run by its owner on their own computer. It turns livestream recordings into short vertical clips and posts them to the owner's own social accounts (YouTube, TikTok, X). It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the owner.

What the app accesses

  • TikTok account data. When the owner connects a TikTok account, the app receives an access token, the account's open id, display name and avatar (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish). These are used only to show which account is connected and to upload videos the owner has approved.
  • Videos and captions. The clips, covers and captions the app uploads are created by the app from the owner's chosen source streams and approved by the owner before posting.
  • Post statistics. View and like counts of the owner's own posts, to show performance inside the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data about any person other than the connected account owner.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer tokens or account data to any third party.
  • It does not track viewers, run analytics on other people, or send data to advertising services.

Where data is stored

All tokens and data stay on the owner's computer, in the app's local folder, in plain files and a local database. Nothing is stored on a server. Tokens are deleted when the account is disconnected in the app (Settings, Accounts, Disconnect) or when the app's secrets folder is deleted.

Third parties

The app calls the TikTok, YouTube and X APIs to post content and read the owner's own post statistics, and the Anthropic API to generate captions and titles from the owner's own transcripts. Only the text needed for that purpose is sent.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the account owner, through the contact address shown on the connected TikTok profile.

ClipEngine is a personal tool and is not affiliated with TikTok, YouTube or X.
