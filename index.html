ClipEngine

A personal desktop tool for one creator

ClipEngine runs on its owner's own computer. It turns recordings of livestreams into short vertical clips, lets the owner review and approve each one, and then posts the approved clips to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and X accounts.

What it does with TikTok

  • The owner signs in with TikTok once, on their own computer.
  • The app uploads videos the owner has approved to that one account, using TikTok's Content Posting API.
  • The app shows the account name so the owner can see which account is connected.

Who uses it

Only its owner. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and stores nothing on a server.

See the privacy policy and the terms of service.
